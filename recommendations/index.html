Infrastructure / control surface

Rabby closes the loop at the signing surface.

What it isRabby is an Ethereum and EVM wallet entry point. In this framework, it is the surface where a user reviews and authorizes the next transaction.
What it can improveTransaction simulation and clearer signing context can make an intended state easier to inspect before approval. A preview is evidence about an expected call, not a safety guarantee.
What it cannot removeSelf-custody, device security, phishing, malicious contracts, chain failures, bridge risk, protocol risk, and the consequences of an authorized signature remain part of the user’s responsibility.

Capital productivity / stablecoin lending

Yield is a service, not a property of the stablecoin.

What Fluid changesSupplying a stablecoin through a lending protocol changes the risk object: the position now depends on the selected asset, network, contracts, liquidity, rate model, and provider controls.
What to verify firstRead the live market, identify where the return comes from, check supply and withdrawal conditions, and distinguish variable lending yield from any separate fixed-rate or incentive product.
What remains yours to controlUse the exact official domain, verify the chain and asset, keep a recovery path, and test the route back out before committing material capital. Never treat a referral as evidence of safety.
